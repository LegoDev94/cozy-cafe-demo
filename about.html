A SMALL PLACE. A LOVELY FEELING.

The idea is simple: good coffee, comforting food, and a place where you can feel at ease.

Cozy Cafe brings together the little things that make a day better. A warm drink between classes. Lunch with someone you haven’t seen in a while. A comfortable corner to finish that chapter.

Whether you’re local to Cadillac or just passing through, there’s room for you here.
